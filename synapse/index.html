How it works

From curious to connected, on your terms.

  1. 01

    Create one free profile

    Tell us the basics, your conditions, medications and interests. Uploading records is optional.

  2. 02

    See potential matches

    We show recruiting studies that may fit you, with only details the site has confirmed.

  3. 03

    You choose who contacts you

    Nothing is shared until you request contact. You name the site and see what it receives.

  4. 04

    The site confirms eligibility

    The research team reaches out and runs formal screening. Taking part is always your choice.

Our promises

We only say what is true.

Clinical research is personal. These rules shape every screen and every message.

You decide who contacts you

A research team only reaches out after you request contact for a specific study.

Request sent. The research team will contact you by phone or email. Taking part is always your choice.
The site confirms eligibility

A potential match means you may qualify. Only the research site can confirm it, through formal screening.

No scores, no guarantees

We never show match percentages or promise enrollment, a cure or results. Study details come from the site, with the date they were confirmed.

Your profile, your control

We ask only for what matching needs. Record upload is optional, and you can edit or delete your profile.
