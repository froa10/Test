The problem

Insurance operations still run on copy, paste and spreadsheets nobody trusts

Data lives in carrier portals, inboxes, the CRM and a dozen workbooks. People shuttle it between them by hand. Every new book of business means hiring more people to do the same repetitive work.

  • 01

    Quotes arrive too late

    A multi-carrier comparison takes days. The prospect has already bound elsewhere.

  • 02

    Policies slip through

    Coverage exists at the carrier but never made it into your system, and neither did the commission.

  • 03

    Premiums you can't see

    Records say "paid" when they aren't. You learn about it from the cancellation notice.

  • 04

    Reconciliation drags on

    Hours of matching statements to portals, with mistakes surfacing after the books close.
