Where things stand

Most insurance operations still depend on people copying data between disconnected tools

Quotes live in carrier portals, confirmations in inboxes, client records in a CRM and everything else in spreadsheets. Each handoff is manual, so the only way to handle more policies has been to add more headcount.

01

Quotes that arrive too late

Building a comparison takes days. The client signs with whoever answers first.

02

Policies that slip through

Business on the carrier side that never reached your system, along with the commission it carries.

03

Premiums nobody verifies

Receipts shown as paid that never cleared, found out only once the cancellation notice lands.

04

Month-end that drags on

Days lost lining up spreadsheets against portal exports, and mistakes surfacing after the books close.
